# Changelog

## 0.1.1

Descriptions checked against published accounts (the Enneagram Institute's type summaries and its pages on types 2, 6 and 9, Integrative Enneagram's introduction, and summaries of the lines and Hornevian groups). Fears, desires, lines and groups all matched; the descriptions gained detail the draft lacked:

- **2:** help can come with unspoken strings, and they may hover or get too involved.
- **4:** shifting moods and underlying self-doubt.
- **6:** under stress they turn competitive and driven, not only image-focused.
- **9:** merging into others' plans, resistance to change and putting things off.
- **5:** keeps thoughts and feelings to themselves (was "inner life").

## 0.1.0

- First release, draft: the nine types (name, centre, core fear and desire, lines of stress and growth), wings, the three centres, the Hornevian and Harmonic groups, and the three instincts, with query functions.
- Structure checked against several published summaries of the Enneagram; the tests check the lines (1-4-2-8-5-7-1 and 3-9-6-3), that growth reverses stress, and that every grouping covers each type once.
- Original descriptions of each type (summary, behaviour, three observable signs, under stress, at ease), marked as a draft for review. The tests check they avoid em dashes and stay off health, diet, weight and eating.
- Passions, fixations and the 27 instinctual subtypes are not included.
